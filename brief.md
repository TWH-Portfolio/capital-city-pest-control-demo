## Build Spec

## Style Direction
Iron Edge industrial preset: zero-radius, deep navy-steel background, brass accent, Oswald display, Archivo body, hard-edge shadows, uppercase labels. Feels like serious trade work, not a generic pest control template.

## References used

### 1. TWH: Karcher Construction Starter
- Hero composition: full-width dark hero with Oswald uppercase display, brass kicker label, hard-edge CTA button
- Section order: Hero > Services grid > Why Us stats > Process steps > Contact
- Copy voice: direct, trade-professional, no fluff, short punchy sentences
- Photo treatment: dark overlay gradients on photo backgrounds, hard-edge frames
- Trust signals: years in business, license numbers, service area specifics
- Conversion path: phone number prominent in nav + brass CTA buttons throughout
- **Apply to this build:** zero-radius cards, brass kicker labels, Oswald uppercase headings, hard-edge button hover with offset shadow, stat blocks with brass left rail

### 2. Orkin.com (national pest control leader)
- Hero composition: strong protective messaging with location-specific headline, clear service CTA above fold
- Section order: Hero with zip-code CTA > Services grid (pest-specific) > Trust bar (stats) > Process steps > Reviews
- Copy voice: solution-oriented, protective ("defend your home"), never fear-mongering
- Photo treatment: clean product/service photography, minimal overlays, high contrast
- Trust signals: years of service, number of technicians, satisfaction guarantee, licensing
- Conversion path: "Get Your Free Quote" repeated, phone number sticky in header
- **Apply to this build:** protective hero messaging angle, pest-specific service cards, 4-step process visualization, prominent phone number, "free inspection" CTA language

### Cross-reference design thesis
Lead with protective, confident hero messaging on the iron-edge dark palette. Use the trade-industrial card grid for services (pest-specific icons). Keep brass accent for all CTAs and kickers. Process section as a 4-step horizontal timeline with brass numbering. Phone number visible at all times. Every CTA routes to either phone or contact form.

## Niche-Specific Notes
- Common Florida pests: termites, palmetto bugs, fire ants, mosquitoes, rodents
- Key differentiators: 5-star Google rating, locally owned, licensed & insured, family-safe treatments
- CTA language: "Get a Free Inspection" / "Schedule Service"
- Messaging angle: protection, prevention, local expertise
